Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

The short version

Pip doesn’t collect any data. There’s no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your habits are stored on your device and, if you choose, in your own private iCloud — which I can’t see.

Who I am

Pip – Habit Tracker (“Pip”) is made by Kunal More, an independent developer. Questions about this policy: mkunal065@gmail.com.

What Pip stores, and where

WhatWhere it’s kept
Your habits, check-ins, notes and settingsOn your device. Shared with Pip’s widgets on the same device so they can show your progress.
The same data, if iCloud sync is onYour private iCloud database, under your Apple Account, so your devices stay in sync. Only you can access it; the developer cannot.
RemindersScheduled as local notifications on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Pip has no servers of its own. Nothing you enter is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What Pip does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of data.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, health data, camera or microphone.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is on by default so your habits are safe if you change or lose your phone. You can turn it off any time in Pip → Settings → iCloud sync; your habits then stay only on that device. iCloud is run by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Siri, Shortcuts and widgets

When you log a habit with Siri or Shortcuts, or check one off from a widget, the change is saved on your device the same way as in the app. Siri requests are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy terms.

Information Apple may share with developers

If you’ve allowed it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share with App Developers, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with me through App Store Connect. These don’t identify you and you can turn them off at any time.

If you email me, I’ll use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them when they’re no longer needed.

Deleting your data

  • One habit: open it, tap ⋯ → Delete habit.
  • Everything on a device: delete the Pip app.
  • Your iCloud copy: iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Storage (Manage) → Pip → Delete data. Do this before deleting the app, or sync will restore your habits on your other devices.

Children

Pip doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example if a future version adds in-app purchases — I’ll update this page and the date above before the change ships.

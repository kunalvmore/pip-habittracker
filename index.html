What it does

Meet Pip

A flame that grows with you

Pip’s crest is your overall streak — from a tiny spark to a bonfire. Miss a day and it dims, but Pip never guilt-trips you. Tomorrow is a fresh start.

Streaks & history

See every good day

Each habit has a calendar, a heat map and its best streak. Forgot to log yesterday? Fix the last seven days in a tap.

Stats

Honest numbers

Week, month and year views show your completion, perfect days, your strongest and weakest weekdays — and how you compare with last time.

Widgets

Check in without opening the app

Home Screen widgets show your streak and let you tick habits off right there. Lock Screen widgets keep your streak one glance away.

Free days

Life happens. Up to two free days a week cover a missed day, so one off day doesn’t wipe out weeks of work.

Build or break

Track habits you’re building and ones you’re quitting. Quitting counts clean days on its own — just log a slip if one happens.

Kind reminders

A morning or evening check-in and a streak-at-risk nudge — and nothing at all once everything is done.

iCloud sync

Your habits follow you to every iPhone and iPad, stored in your own private iCloud.

Siri & Shortcuts

“Log Read in Pip.” Check habits off with your voice or from any Shortcut.

iPhone & iPad

Made for both, with a roomy split view on iPad, plus light and dark mode everywhere.

Private by design

Your habits are yours.

Pip collects no data at all. Everything stays on your device and in your own private iCloud — the developer can’t see it. Read the privacy policy.

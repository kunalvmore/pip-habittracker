Frequently asked questions

How do streaks work?

A habit’s streak grows by one each scheduled day you complete it. Today never breaks a streak — it waits until the day is over.

For “some days” habits, the other days are rest days and never break the streak. For “N × a week” habits, the streak counts weeks.

Pip’s flame on Today follows your overall streak: a day counts when every daily habit scheduled that day is done (or covered by a free day).

What are free days?

A free day covers a missed scheduled day so your streak stays alive. Choose None, 1 or 2 per week in Settings → Free days. They’re applied automatically to the oldest miss first. A free day keeps the streak but doesn’t add to it.

I checked in after midnight and it counted for the wrong day

Go to Settings → Day ends at and choose 3 AM. Check-ins before 3 AM will then count for the day before.

Can I fix a day I forgot to log?

Yes, for the last 7 days. Open the habit, tap the day in its calendar, then tap Log (or Clear).

How do “Breaking” habits work?

Habits you’re quitting count every day as clean on their own — no tap needed. If you slip, tap the Clean pill on Today and choose “I slipped today”. Only that habit’s count restarts. Slips never affect your main streak or Pip’s flame.

How do I archive, restore or delete a habit?

Archive hides a habit from Today, widgets and reminders but keeps its history. Archive from the habit’s ⋯ menu, its Edit screen, or by long-pressing it on Today.

Restore it from Settings → Archived habits → Unarchive. Its history and best streak are still there.

Delete removes the habit and all its check-ins for good, after a confirmation.

My habits aren’t syncing between devices
  1. Make sure every device is signed in to the same Apple Account, and that iCloud is allowed for Pip (iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → apps using iCloud → Pip).
  2. In Pip, check Settings → iCloud sync is on, on each device.
  3. Open Pip on both devices with an internet connection and give it a minute — sync happens in the background.
  4. Still stuck? Email me with the device models; your habits stay safe on each device meanwhile.
How do I add a widget or Lock Screen widget?

Home Screen: touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Pip and pick Streak, Habit or Today. The Today widget lets you check habits off without opening the app.

Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen → the widget area, then choose a Pip widget.

I’m not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Pip → Notifications, and that Settings → Daily check-in is on in Pip. Focus modes can hold notifications back. Pip also skips reminders once everything for the day is done.

Can I use Pip with Siri or Shortcuts?

Yes. Say “Log habit name in Pip”, or find Pip’s actions in the Shortcuts app.

Is Pip free?

Yes. Pip has no ads, no account and no in-app purchases.

What data does Pip collect?

None. Your habits stay on your device and, if you use iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud. Read the Privacy Policy.
